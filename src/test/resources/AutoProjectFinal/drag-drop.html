🖱️ Drag & Drop

Practice drag and drop interactions using Selenium's ActionChains.

💡 Selenium Drag & Drop

from selenium.webdriver.common.action_chains import ActionChains

Method 1 — drag_and_drop:
ActionChains(driver).drag_and_drop(source, target).perform()

Method 2 — drag_and_drop_by_offset:
ActionChains(driver).drag_and_drop_by_offset(source, x_offset, y_offset).perform()

Method 3 — click_and_hold + move + release:
ActionChains(driver).click_and_hold(source).move_to_element(target).release().perform()
